Our Story

Fresh from the market, straight to your table

B6ga3e began as a small family kitchen with one simple belief: great food brings people together. Today, our chefs blend time-honoured recipes with modern technique to create dishes that feel both familiar and exciting.

We source locally every morning, bake our bread in-house and cook over open flame — because you can taste the difference.
