    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Quick answers to the things guests ask us most.

    Do I need a reservation? +

    Walk-ins are always welcome, but we recommend booking for Friday and weekend evenings, and for groups of six or more.

    Do you cater for dietary requirements? +

    Yes — we have vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options on every menu. Let us know about allergies when booking and our chefs will take care of you.

    Can I host a private event? +

    Absolutely. Our private dining room seats up to 40 guests, and we offer full buyouts for larger celebrations. Choose "Private event" in the form above.

    Do you offer delivery or takeaway? +

    Yes, we deliver within 8 km and takeaway orders are ready in about 20 minutes. Call us to place an order.

    Is there parking available? +

    Free guest parking is available behind the restaurant, plus street parking nearby.
